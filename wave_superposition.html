Physics Reference
Wave superposition
Ψ = Σ A·sin(kₙx − ωₙt)
Adding N waves with slightly different k and ω values creates a localized wave packet — the quantum mechanical model of a particle.
Phase vs Group velocity
vₚ = ω/k · vg = dω/dk
Phase velocity is how fast the ripples move. Group velocity is how fast the packet (information) travels. vg = vₚ + k·(dvₚ/dk)
Uncertainty Principle
Δx · Δk ≥ ½
Narrowing the packet in position (large Δk) spreads it in momentum space. This is the Heisenberg uncertainty principle: Δx · Δp ≥ ħ/2
